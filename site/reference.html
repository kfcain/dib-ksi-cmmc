No-L1 option
No-L1 option active (purist reading): CMMC Level 1 is deleted from the program, and Class A, the entry class built around it, dies with it. Nothing replaces them: the FCI floor is the FAR 52.204-21 contract clause alone. The 15 safeguards still bind on every federal contract, but no CMMC assessment, affirmation, or class apparatus attaches to them. What is actually measured the unit of assurance

A KSI says what must be true. A measurement is the reading that shows it is true right now, and it carries a shelf life. Past that window the claim it supported reports stale rather than passing.

Coverage requirements are universal, not statistical. There is no aggregate percentage bar, because no authority publishes one. Every in-scope subject must be covered; an uncovered subject is allowed only by an exception carrying a justification and an expiry date. A finding names the workstation instead of averaging it away.

How a reading is produced methods, not a product list

This framework keeps no list of approved tools and endorses none. A measurement is fed by whatever can read the state and emit a conforming reading: a vendor API, a webhook, an agent, a SIEM query, a CI job, an export, a script somebody wrote on a Tuesday. What is constrained is the shape of the reading and the assurance properties of the method behind it.

Three things get confused constantly. The criteria source says what good looks like — a secure configuration baseline, a benchmark, a hardening guide, a vulnerability catalogue, or your own policy. The method is how state is retrieved. The implementation is what you happen to run. A baseline is not a collector, and a tool that ships a baseline is not the same thing as the baseline it ships.

Rules the model cannot break and what each one prevents

Most frameworks are lists of things you ought to do. These are ten rules the model enforces on itself — properties that stay true no matter what anyone types into it, because the software refuses to produce a result that violates them.

That distinction matters more than it sounds. A rule you can override under deadline pressure is a suggestion. If you cannot mark something as handled by your cloud provider without three specific conditions being true, you cannot quietly get that wrong at 4pm on a Friday. The rule is doing the remembering instead of you.

What "enforced" means here. The framework ships with an automated check for each rule. Every change runs the whole set, and a change that breaks one is rejected rather than published — the same way a spreadsheet refuses to show a total when a formula points at a deleted cell. Nobody has to notice the violation for it to be caught.
